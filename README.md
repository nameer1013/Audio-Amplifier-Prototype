# Audio Amplifier Circuit Prototype involving PCB assembly, connector integration, and electronic circuit implementation.

Overview:
Designed and assembled a low-power audio amplifier prototype using PCB implementation, soldered circuit connections, and discrete electronic components.
The project involved practical hardware assembly, connector integration, power supply wiring, signal-routing implementation, and basic circuit troubleshooting.

Objectives:
Develop practical understanding of electronic circuit implementation
Perform hardware assembly and wiring integration
Understand signal routing and power supply connections
Gain hands-on exposure to prototype-based engineering systems

Components Used:
Audio amplifier IC
PCB board
9V battery supply
Audio input/output connectors
Switches and resistors
Connecting wires and soldered joints

Engineering Concepts:
Basic analog electronics
Circuit implementation
Prototype assembly
Connector integration
Troubleshooting and testing
